# Cross Player 1.3.0

Cross Player can now stop playback on its own, so you can fall asleep without waking up to the next episode.

## Sleep timer

### Set a timer
1. Open the **Cross Player** queue.
2. Click the **moon icon** in the queue header.
3. Pick a preset — 5, 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes — or choose **Custom…** and enter any number of minutes.

While the timer runs, the moon becomes a live countdown in the queue header, for example **🌙 24m**. In the last minute it switches to seconds and turns orange. Click the countdown at any time to change the duration or turn it off.

### Stop after the current file
Choose **End of current file** to finish exactly what you are watching or listening to and then stop. This also skips **Auto-play Next**, so a new episode will not start while you are asleep.

### Good to know
- The timer keeps running even if you close the queue view; playback still pauses at the deadline.
- If you pause and come back, the countdown continues from where it is. It is a wall-clock timer.
- Timers are per device and last until you restart Obsidian. They are not synced between devices.
- Pick a new duration at any time to replace the old one.

## New commands
- **Set Sleep Timer** — opens the queue and shows the sleep timer menu.
- **Cancel Sleep Timer** — appears only while a timer is active.

## New setting
- **Sleep Timer Presets** — choose which durations appear in the sleep timer menu. Leave it empty to only offer **Custom…** and **End of current file**.
